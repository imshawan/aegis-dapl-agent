# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in the **Aegis DAPL Agent** project and our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our community include:

- **Demonstrating empathy and kindness** toward other people and automated agents operating in collaborative developer workspaces.
- **Being respectful** of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- **Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback** during code reviews and incident post-mortems.
- **Accepting responsibility** and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from the experience.
- **Focusing on what is best** not just for us as individuals, but for the overall security and stability of the community and ecosystem.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information (such as a physical or email address, or API credentials/secrets) without their explicit permission.
- **Malicious Agent Interaction**: Attempting to bypass the agent security firewall, inject prompt hijacks, or intentionally trigger Denial of Service (DoS) ceilings in community environments.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Autonomous Agent & Bot Governance

As a project pioneering **Advanced Agentic Coding** and autonomous remediation, we hold both human contributors and automated systems to high standards of operational safety:
- **Transparent Identification**: Automated bots, scripts, or subagent integrations interacting with our GitHub repository or community channels must be clearly labeled as automated entities.
- **Respect for Review Workflows**: Automated pull requests generated by agents must pass our established commitlint, gitleaks, and native test harness checks before requesting human review.
- **Rate Limiting & Resource Fairness**: Contributors operating automated testing loops must respect rate limits and DoS ceilings to ensure fair access to community CI/CD and API resources.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Community leaders have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, pull requests, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned to this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces (including GitHub repositories, pull requests, issue trackers, and Slack discussion channels), and also applies when an individual or automated service officially represents the project in public spaces.

Examples of representing our community include using an official e-mail address, posting via an official social media account, or acting as an appointed representative at an online or offline event.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the community leaders responsible for enforcement at **[github@imshawan.dev](mailto:github@imshawan.dev)** (or by contacting the project maintainers directly). All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

All community leaders are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.
